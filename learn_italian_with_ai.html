🏛️ Grammar basics

Start with two of the most important Italian verbs.

Lesson 2

Essere — to be

Use essere to talk about identity, descriptions, and where someone is from.

Io sono
I amsono
Tu sei
You aresei
Lui / Lei è
He / She isè

Avere — to have

Use avere to express possession, age, and some common needs.

Io ho
I haveho
Tu hai
You havehai
Lui / Lei ha
He / She hasha

Remember!

Sono italiana. = I am Italian.

Ho vent'anni. = I am twenty years old. (Literally: I have twenty years.)
